Every part, accounted for.

From the monocoque to the smallest fastener, the Vantage GT is composed of 4,218 individual parts. Each one is engineered, tested, and traceable.

Chassis

A monocoque of carbon & aluminum.

Woven carbon fiber tub bonded to extruded aluminum subframes. Torsional rigidity of 41,000 Nm/deg in a structure that weighs less than 96 kg.

Dry Weight1,318 kg
Rigidity41,000 Nm/°

Twin-turbo V8. Naturally aspirated soul.

A 4.0-liter flat-crank V8 mounted amidships. 612 horsepower. Rev-matched downshifts. A soundtrack tuned by a composer, not an engineer.
